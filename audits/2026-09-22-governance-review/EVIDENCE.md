## Reproduction and limits

[README reproduction order](README.md#4-reproduction) regenerates ignored source/chain intermediates. The compact index checker works without those raw inputs; rebuilding this index requires them. Archived hashes identify the original inputs but are not proof of completeness or authenticity. RPC/explorer availability, log completeness and matching source dependencies remain assumptions. Receipt checks validate returned logs, not the absence of omitted events. A fresh clone contains references and reproduction code, not the original raw archive.
